A/V sync test

  1. Volume up, silent mode off, brightness up. Disconnect Bluetooth headphones so the beep comes out of the phone.
  2. On the computer, look at the control panel preview. It switches to Camera (the plain webcam picture without the cut-out) when you press Start. If it still shows your background, click Camera there.
  3. Press Start, then turn this phone around: screen facing the webcam lens, 20 to 40 cm away, speaker toward the webcam.
  4. In the Camera preview, check that the whole phone screen is in the picture and flashing. Hold still and keep quiet for about 15 seconds.

The screen flashes white and beeps at the same moment once a second. mattecast measures how much later the camera sees the flash than the microphone hears the beep.
