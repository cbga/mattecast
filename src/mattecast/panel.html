Microphone

Audio forwarding is off. It turns on by itself when the camera has a microphone and the capture card's HDMI audio port is found; otherwise pass --audio-in and --audio-out.

Your phone cannot reach mattecast yet: it only listens on this computer. Stop it (Ctrl+C) and start it again with --control-host 0.0.0.0 (phone on the same Wi-Fi) or --control-host $(tailscale ip -4) (Tailscale), then open this panel again.
The preview now shows Camera, the plain webcam picture without the cut-out, so you can see the phone. Keep it on Camera during the test.
  1. On your phone: volume up, silent mode off, brightness up, Bluetooth headphones off.
  2. Open the /sync page on the phone and press Start.
  3. Turn the phone around: its screen faces the webcam lens, 20 to 40 cm away, speaker toward the webcam.
  4. Check in the preview that the whole phone screen is in the picture and flashing. Hold still and stay quiet for about 15 seconds.

Quality

Changing the model resolution re-detects the person. At 30 fps keep processing time under 33 ms.
